# 53 — Thinking is most of what a turn streams, and none of it is live

> _This was generated by AI during triage._

**What to build:** `thinking_delta` handled on the streaming path, so the window
`--include-partial-messages` exists to fill is actually filled.

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Found by:** the 2026-07-28 parity audit, measured —
`.scratch/rust-server-tauri/2026-07-28-cli-stream-audit/` finding 2.

## What happens

`crate::protocol`'s `Delta` handles `text_delta` only. `thinking_delta`,
`input_json_delta` and `signature_delta` fall to `Delta::Unknown` and produce
nothing. This is **declared** in the code, so it is a known choice rather than an
oversight — but the measurement changes the argument for it.

Across two short turns against the real binary: **23 thinking deltas against 7
text deltas.**

`--include-partial-messages` was kept, against upstream, on the explicit ground
that streaming _"is the only thing filling the first two seconds of a turn"_. On a
reasoning model the first seconds of a turn **are** thinking — so the window the
flag exists to fill is precisely the window it does not fill. The flag is paying
its cost and not collecting its benefit.

## What is and is not lost

Nothing is lost. The thinking arrives whole in the buffered `assistant` message and
renders as a row. What is missing is liveness: it lands in one jump at the end
rather than appearing as it is produced.

That makes this a smaller ticket than the line count suggests — the rendering
already exists, the reconciliation between deltas and the buffered message already
exists for text (ticket 11), and this is the same mechanism applied to a second
block type.

## Key interfaces

- The `Delta` enum in `crate::protocol` — a `thinking_delta` arm.
- The delta-versus-buffered reconciliation from ticket 11. Thinking blocks must
  reconcile the same way text does; a duplicated thinking block on every turn is a
  worse outcome than the current one.
- `signature_delta` accompanies thinking blocks. Decide whether it is needed for
  reconciliation or can stay unhandled — but decide it, rather than leaving it in
  the catch-all by default (see 44).

## Acceptance

- [ ] Thinking appears progressively during a turn rather than arriving whole at
      the end.
- [ ] The buffered `assistant` message does not produce a second copy of the
      thinking that already streamed.
- [ ] An interrupted turn leaves no partial thinking block orphaned in the log.
- [ ] A fixture containing thinking deltas exists. None of the eighteen committed
      ones do.
- [ ] `input_json_delta` is either handled or explicitly declared ignored — not
      left silent by default.

## Out of scope

- The `thinking_tokens` counter, which is the other half of the same window — 43.
- Removing `--include-partial-messages`. It stays; that is a settled divergence
  from upstream, recorded in the ledger §4.
